# PlantX components

## Where

| Kind | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Shared UI | `src/components/<Name>/` |
| Feature UI | `src/features/<feature>/components/<Name>/` |
| Page shell | `src/pages/<Name>Page/` |
| Browser utility | `src/lib/` (no styles/story) |

## Each component

- `<Name>.tsx` — the component
- `<Name>.styles.ts` — styled-components next to it
- `<Name>.stories.tsx` — at least one story

Pages compose features. They do not own repeated form controls.

## Extract when

A pattern appears twice or more (same Field + Select + "Choose", same admin header + tabs). Pull it into a named component with styles and a story.

## Examples

- `AdminPage` — admin check, tabs
- `CatalogSelect` — labeled catalog select with choose option
- `PhotoIdentify` — `max` photos (default `MAX_PLANT_PHOTOS`, 3), each scanned separately; Add Plant passes `ADD_PLANT_UPLOAD_LIMIT` (1). Renders `AiScan` for the selected photo. A single photo has the remove control on that scan and no thumbnail or hint under it. More than one photo keeps the strip of slots with stickers. Stories keep the multi-photo frames.
- `PhotoCheckSticker` — tilted "test passed" stamp with one photo's AI result (`sm` on thumbs, `md` on big photos)
- `PhotoChecks` — a plant's photos with numbered thumbs and stickers (review, passport activity, admin preview)
- `AddPlantWizard` — stepped flow on `Stepper` + `ChoiceChips`, inside a fixed smaller `AddPlantDialog` (the step body scrolls, the footer stays). The first step is Add photo, and a photo is required. Fill in manually and a bluish Continue with AI sit together; both enable once a photo is added. AI runs only from that button, once per photo: after a scan (answered or failed) Continue with AI stays disabled until the photo is changed, and a new photo waits for the button again. A plant answer fills the other steps and always slides to review. Once a photo is in, every stepper step and Next are open (not while AI is reading); only Save waits for the required fields. Before a photo the footer says to add one, and the clear-photo line hides once a photo is in. The AI answer (name, confidence) stays on that review step; going back to the photo shows the photo only. Category and subcategory chips show a small catalog photo and the name. On a phone (≤640px, `useMediaQuery`) a tap selects, and the “pick the category” lead stays. Wider, that lead and the footer hint are hidden; hover shows a small clickable note (scientific name and light); clicking that note opens `CatalogPreview` with the rest of the catalog card (a variety leads with its own photo and code) and no Choose button. Clicking the chip only selects it. Other selects directly, with no popup. No chosen-class card sits between the rows. Every option row (category, subcategory, size, stage, traits) is one sideways row (`ChoiceChips` `scroll`) with small ‹ › arrows while more sits past an edge, on every width; the chosen option is scrolled into view. No row is ever gray or disabled: each shows every option it can have, and a parent choice narrows its children. With no category yet, the subcategory row lists every variety (its category as the hint), and picking one picks its category. Stage always lists every stage in the catalog (size and variety do not narrow it) and is never picked for the grower, even with one option. Traits that don't apply to the category or variety are not shown. After an AI answer only what the AI answered is filled (no first-choice or single-option fill); the rest is flagged for the owner. An AI value stays visible even when an earlier field is empty (e.g. a growth form with no size). The Specs and Save hints name the empty fields. Review lists size, stage, and every required trait with Edit; an empty one is tinted and reads Missing, the title becomes Almost ready, and a warning banner (AI couldn't fill these) has one button per empty field that opens its step. After an AI answer, or once Review was seen, each empty required field is tinted on its step (`ChoiceChips` `missing`, "AI couldn't fill this") and the stepper marks those steps with a warning dot (`Stepper` `open` / `flagged`). A chosen class shows a small white catalog mark. On review the plant photo is at most 240px wide when stacked, the catalog photo is a small mark, and the `IdentifyBadge` sits on the photo. The dialog has no title or intro. The photo step has no heading; the clear-photo line sits under the photo at every width, including under 420px. The stepper names the step and shows every step name at every width. Under 420px of the dialog the other step headings and info banners hide; a warning banner with Use AI's answer stays. An AI answer with no catalog category, saved as Other, still counts as an AI answer, but its badge is blue and says AI recognized it (Not in catalog), not AI verified. When the species step failed, the AI answer calls the name Name, not Species. Location is not a step: the plant copies the greenhouse place from the account dialog (`GreenhousePlace`), Unknown until the owner changes it. The catalog area trait is not required and is not saved. A saved plant is named like a plant (category, plus the variety when one is chosen, e.g. "Pothos Golden"; Other uses the AI name), not by its class code. A signed-out guest can open the form and fill it in. Continue with AI and Save open `AuthDialog` (portaled above this dialog) and do not call identify or create a plant.
- `GreenhousePlace` — owner location select in `AccountDialog` (the caller labels it; pass `id`). Default Unknown when the region is empty or already unknown.
- `IdentifyBadge` — AI verified / edited / manual mark for a plant (card, review, passport activity). Compact chips share one height; the compact AI chip is the AI blue (`theme.colors.aiBlue`) with ✦, like the passport stamps.
- `AiFieldStamp` — per-field AI provenance on the passport and Add Plant review: ✦ kept, ✎ changed; hover, focus or tap opens a styled tooltip with the AI's suggestion. A category changed by hand is the only mark on that plant. The passport identity head does not show it; the stamp on the photo stays.
- `ApisPanel` — presentational; page loads providers and suggestions. Tabs are the pipeline stages (plant check, species, catalog fields). Each stage is Ready or Mock on its own, and a Mock change stays on that stage. The status badge follows that stage's response when the API is up. A failed save is shown on the stage. Enabled and Ready with no API key shows a warning on the stage, and the Ready option reads Ready (no API key). The species stage notes that its Not in catalog answer is only a hint; Catalog fields decides. The catalog-fields mock uses the same chips as Add Plant: category, subcategory, size, stage, and that category's traits. `IdentifyPlayground` uses the same stage controls for a pipeline run.
- `AdminSection` — admin card with heading, lead, side note
- `Pager` (`src/components`) — previous / next with `PAGE_SIZE` (10). `AdminTable` and the market use `usePaged`. A single page hides the control. `anchor: 'end'` opens a timeline on the latest page.
- `InfiniteScroll` (`src/components`) — same page size, loaded when the edge scrolls into view. News grows downward. Greenhouse activity opens on the latest rows and loads older rows when its thread scrolls up.
- `FilterChips` (`src/components`) — filter chips with an optional icon and count. Greenhouse filters and the Home feed (wider than 900px) use it. Under 560px of the page they use the Tasks phone pill (13px, neutral fill, forest when selected) and wrap; between that and 720px they stay one scrolling row.
- `IconToggle` — icon segmented pill; every option has a tooltip. `floating` fixes it in the middle above the bottom nav and shows the chosen option's name next to its icon. Greenhouse scope and the home feed on a phone use it.
- `RefreshButton`, `PullToRefresh` (`src/components`) — refresh icon that spins while busy; phone pull-to-refresh for a window-scrolled page.
- `Switch` (`src/components`) — on/off `role="switch"` with busy spinner; System and APIs admin toggles
- `SuggestionEditorDialog` — admin catalog entry form for category, subcategory, properties, and both photos. Requests opens it from an open suggestion (Review & approve; saving adds it to the catalog and marks the row added) or empty from New catalog entry (saved straight into the catalog). A member's note shows above the form. A suggestion with `draft.categoryId` is a new variety: the category fields are replaced by "New variety of X" and `applyCatalogSuggestion` adds the subcategory to that category.
- `RequestsPanel` — Admin → Requests. Applications are one Server-style table, suggested categories are another, with New catalog entry above it. Each suggestion shows its source (Scan / Member); the expanded row shows who suggested it, the note, and hits. Open rows sit with added and declined rows. Each table selects, expands, and pages by 10. Mock mode reads the browser's suggestions.
- `SuggestPlantDialog` (`src/features/catalog/components/`) — the Catalog's Suggest a plant form: A new plant / A new variety (then a scroll row of categories), name, optional scientific name, optional photo and note. It warns live when the name is already in the catalog (with a link) and disables Send. Sent shows a thank-you that says it is pending in the catalog. Validation is shared with the server (`catalogSuggest.ts`).
- `PendingSuggestionCard` — a member's own open suggestion in the Catalog: WikiCard size, dashed border, faded photo with a Pending review pill, and From your scan / Suggested by you. Not a link.
- `GuestHomeIntro` (`src/features/discover/components/`) — the signed-out Home: eyebrow, title, three tappable steps with a progress bar on the playing one, and a stage (example photo, scan line → AI chip → care chip). Logical properties only, container queries for the stacked phone layout, all motion off under reduced motion.
- `HoldNotice` — not launched: room tabs. A page under maintenance (`pageId` from `PageGate`): names that page and links only to pages that are live. API down (no `pageId`): the message alone.
- `ModalDialog` (`src/components`) — small form or confirm dialog: title, lead, body, footer actions; portaled above other dialogs, Escape and backdrop close it, a bottom sheet on a phone. Admin dialogs (moderation confirm, user edit, scans) use it.
- `InlineEdit` / `EditPencil` (`src/features/greenhouse/components/InlineEdit`) — edit one passport value in place: the owner and the admin see a pencil named for the field ("Edit Size"); the editor (text, note or option chips) replaces the value with Save and Cancel right under it; nothing saves on change; a refused save keeps the editor with the error. The passport edits name, note, size, stage and traits this way; there is no edit popup. One field is open at a time. Editable in `PassportDialog` (embedded + `dialog`); only the inert admin placement previews (embedded, no dialog) stay read-only.
- `ScanQuotaNote` / `MyScanAllowance` — today's AI scans as a small tappable tile ("AI scans today", "2 of 3 left", one segment per scan; tap shows the reset time, open at zero, gray not warm); the admin sees Unlimited and is never blocked. Shown on Add Plant's photo step, the account dialog, admin Scans and the greenhouse header (`GreenhouseLevelCard` `aside`).
- `ModerationPanel` (Admin → Moderation) owns every action on users, plants and activities: edit, scans, disable / enable, preapproved, verify greenhouse, hide / show, delete / restore — in the same `AdminTable` chrome as Server (section head with count, expandable detail rows). `ServerPanel` is read-only; its user preview links to Moderation (`?type=&q=`). A plant's Edit there opens its passport.
- `SkeletonBar` (`src/components/Skeleton`) — shimmering bar or circle; feature skeletons are built from it. `GuestCurtain` — blurs a placeholder layout under a floating card (guest), or shows it plain while loading (member). See plantx-audience.
- `LoaderShell` — table placeholder while a live slice or admin list is still loading. Pass `busy` while a slice is in flight after the API is already up. `compact` is the rail/widget size (no skeleton rows). Server sections and System page/feature rows use `useSectionFetch`: opening one shows the loader and that header stays open until the response. A failed slice shows unavailable and can close. APIs uses the loader for providers and history. `GreenhouseLure` uses the compact shell. The greenhouse shelf, Global directory, level card and Home feed do not: while their data is in flight they show their own skeletons (`CollectionBoard skeleton="loading"`, `GreenhouseDirectorySkeleton`, `GreenhouseLevelSkeleton`, `FeedUpdateSkeleton`), so the header stays when you arrive from another page. Mock mode skips it.
- `FloatChip` — mobile-only fixed chip (draggable, tap opens sheet). Home uses `HomeMobileFloats` for greenhouse + Needs today.
- `Avatar` — colored disc with the grower's icon. The seed is the default. Further icons unlock by greenhouse level.
- `AccountDialog` — the account bubble. Private account name and email, public nickname (Save shows when it changes) and icon. Admin, language, and sign out live here. There is no profile route.
