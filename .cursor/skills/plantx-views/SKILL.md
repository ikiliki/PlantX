# PlantX views

## Reuse

- Put UI in `src/components`, `src/features/<feature>/components`, or `src/pages`.
- Each component has a sibling `*.styles.ts` (styled-components) and a story.
- A landing mock, rail, or card uses the same component as the route. Do not draw a second fake version of a table, deck, feed, or catalog.
- The catalog is the wiki route (`/wiki`, feature id `wiki`). The tab label is Catalog. Its menu lists All, then rarity headings collapsed until clicked. The catalog page has no title block. The contents table starts collapsed. Under it, Common starts open and the other rarities stay closed until clicked. Home `WikiRail` stays off the phone (hidden under 900px of the home shell) and on the wider rails. Its rows open `CatalogPreview` (photos, this season’s light and water); the full seasons stay on the article. On a phone that preview is a 75svh sheet with `SheetGrip`.

## View

Shared type: `ComponentView` in `src/theme/view.ts` — `'page' | 'widget'`.

- `page` is the route. It can show the header, map, table of contents, and rails.
- `widget` is the same component in a smaller frame. Pass `view="widget"`. Show the same children, fewer rows, and drop chrome that only belongs on the route (page title block, map, toc).
- Default the prop to `'page'` so existing call sites stay full pages.
- The landing shows the app through stills in `public/landing/`, listed once in `src/features/landing/landingShots.ts` and framed by `DeviceFrame` (browser or phone). Do not mount a live page there. Sections, in order: hero (Sign up / Go to app, desk + phone still; the bar’s top-right action is also Go to app, opening `/greenhouse`; log in stays under the hero buttons and in `LandingJoin`), proof row, `LandingAi` (Add Plant's four AI steps, auto-advancing until picked; under 900px of the landing they are header tabs on one frame, with the chosen step's title under the tabs and the still filling the frame), `LandingTour` (the interactive dashboard: area tabs + Desktop/Phone switch; phone widths start on phone stills; greenhouse, passport, tasks, and home have phone stills; catalog does not, so Phone stays off there), `LandingJoin` (the real `AuthPanel` sign-up card). Only show live features; market and rank stay off the landing while they are not ready. Landing sections size with `@container landing` on `LandingPage` `Main`, not on `Page`, so fixed children keep the viewport. Re-shoot stills from mock mode as persona Maya (Add Plant with a pothos photo: the mock AI always answers golden pothos). Scratch capture scripts and pngs stay on disk under `scripts/tmp-*` and are gitignored. The greenhouse still shows the level ring, rank, and XP.

`GradeStack` uses `tab | page | widget`. The widget deck is `min(320px, 100%)` wide with a 3:4 ratio. Do not size a deck with `100svh` inside a card.

## Size

- Layout that can sit in a card follows the container. Put `container-type: inline-size` on the parent and use `@container` on the children. A container does not query itself.
- Widths use `min(…px, 100%)`, `minmax(0, 1fr)`, and `min-width: 0`. Text wraps. Do not clip a title with `overflow: hidden` on a scaled page.
- A grid card that holds tables, chip rows, or previews sets `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`; an implicit `auto` column lets one wide child push the card past the screen. Chip rows (`Segmented`, filters) stay one row and scroll sideways. Admin table expanded rows pin to the visible scroller (`ExpandBody`). A name in Server that points at another table opens a popup in place: a plant opens `PassportDialog`, a user opens the user preview. It does not scroll that other section open.
- Check phones with `scripts/mobile-audit.mjs` (mock dev server on 5174; 280–1000px, routes and dialogs); it lists anything poking past the viewport or its own card.
- Do not `transform: scale()` a full page into a frame. The landing used to render a viewport-wide tree and scale it; that clipped type and made container queries read the wrong ancestor.
- Viewport `@media` is only for the app shell (top bar, bottom nav). Embedded components use `@container`.
- Listing rows: the table scroller is the container. At `max-width: 720px` the row keeps photo, name, health, price, and change.
- Home columns follow `DiscoverPage` `Shell`. The Home feed has `FilterChips` (All, Activities, Tasks, with icons; `?feed=`). It opens on Tasks. Under 900px those three leave the top and become an icon-only `IconToggle` floating in the middle above the bottom nav (same pill as greenhouse scope); Tasks stays selected. That pill, and the public-greenhouse back arrow, portal to `document.body` so the page-enter transform on `main` cannot carry them down from mid-screen. Tasks are a new plant (`added`) and completed care (water, photo). Activities are everything else. `?feed=all` and `?feed=activities` select the other chips. Refresh: wider than 900px has a `RefreshButton` at the end of that chip row; the mobile shell pulls down at the top (`PullToRefresh`, which also turns off the browser's own pull-to-reload). Both call `reloadSlice` for updates, todos and plants (`loadSlice` keeps a loaded slice cached for the session). A `ScrollTopButton` on the start side (the Needs-today chip owns the end corner) returns to the top once scrolled. Below 900px the left rail hides; phones get a fixed, draggable `HomeMobileFloats` (`FloatChip`) for Needs you today — drag to move, tap opens a sheet popup (no dismiss). The greenhouse lure float is off the phone for now. Pinned greenhouses stay on the desktop home rail only. No inline MobileLure / MobileTodo / friends-world filter on the feed. `GreenhouseLure` always shows 3 shelf slots (plants, then empty + cards: 0→3, 1→2, 2→1, 3→0) and a compact `LoaderShell` while the plants slice is in flight.
- A grower popup (`GreenhousePublic` compact, from the news avatar or `/sellers/:id`) keeps the name header and lists that greenhouse in a vertical scroller. The window shows one fewer plant than they have, unless there are 0 or 1, and never more than 3. Go to greenhouse stays under the list on every width and opens `/greenhouse/:ownerId` — the public greenhouse page, not a profile. The name in the popup does not navigate. A plant in that list or on the public greenhouse opens `PassportDialog`.
- Market list and map follow `MarketPage` `Board` at 960px. Under that, `MarketSplit` shows one pane at a time (list first), switched by the icon-only `MarketViewToggle`, and the incoming pane slides in; 960px and up shows both and hides the toggle. The map mounts only when visible. On a phone the search box takes the first row and the market filter pills are one sideways-scrolling row (`Pills`) ending with the list / map toggle, and each pill menu opens as a bottom sheet (`PillMenu`), because the row would clip a dropdown. The coming-soon market has no title, subtitle, Charts link or ticker: a "Coming soon" pill, quiet filters (`MarketSearch inactive`) with the working toggle at the end of the pills row, and blurred (masked) sample listings from the mock seed when there are no real ones (`ListingTable` / `ListingMap` `source`). The passport Market tab, while the market is not ready, shows a blurred `StockChart` of seeded stand-in prices under the "coming soon" tape. The Tasks page heading is the name only and hides under 720px of `Page`. The greenhouse has no visible title: its level card is the header.
- `GreenhouseLevelCard` is the header of both greenhouse pages (yours and `/greenhouse/:ownerId`): full width. At 640px and wider the progress bar sits under the level block, and plant and care sit just to the right of that block with a gap. The “Greenhouse level” label is not shown. Narrower, it stacks: level, bar, then the counts. The grower's `Avatar` is pinned to the level ring; the page title / grower name stays only as a visually hidden h1. It shows the ring, rank, XP bar, plant and care counts, and a gray "?" at the end of the level line, the same size as that line, that opens the rules. While those numbers are still loading it keeps the same card and shows `LoaderShell`, so the header is on screen before the data arrives. There is no wallet card; a future greenhouse tab takes that place. A level rise plays a one-time "Level up!" pill (last seen level in localStorage).
- There is no profile page. The account bubble opens `AccountDialog`: the account name and email are private, the nickname is the public name, and Save appears when that nickname changes. A globe marks public and a lock marks private. Avatars are icons. Everyone starts with the seed; more icons unlock by greenhouse level (`avatarIcons`). `/profile` redirects home.
- Plant passport stacks with `@container` on `Frame` (not viewport media), so a narrow dialog stacks on a wide window. On a phone the sheet is 75svh, with a `SheetGrip` on top; drag the pill down to close. `html` keeps `overflow-y: scroll` so locking scroll for any popup leaves the scrollbar in place and the page behind it does not widen. The photo is a sibling of the details and the activity. Under 760px those three stack in that order; the aside thumb hides; the photo keeps its AI stamp and the identity head has no AI badge; the green wash runs top to bottom.
- Greenhouse scope is Mine or Global (`scope` query). On a phone the leaf / globe toggle is a small pill floating in the middle above the bottom nav (`GreenhouseScope` / `IconToggle` `floating`), so the level card leads the page, then the filters, then the plants. On a grower's public greenhouse it becomes `GreenhouseBack`, a round back arrow (it narrows from the pill and the arrow swings in) that always leads to the public greenhouses list (Global). Every link to a greenhouse, your own included, opens the public page (`greenhouseHref`); the Greenhouse tab stays your private board. The public greenhouse page has no "In the greenhouse" heading; the grower popup keeps it. From the desktop header, the Greenhouse tab dropdown carries Mine and Global. Global is search, then the verified greenhouses (always on top, even when the query misses them, with a blue `VerifiedStamp`), then the other grower cards loaded with `InfiniteSentinel`; both groups sort by greenhouse level, highest first (XP, then name, break ties). Each card shows `LevelBadge` (level ring with the grower's avatar pinned) and "Level n · Rank" under the name; levels come from `useGreenhouseLevels` (`GET /api/users/levels` outside mock mode). Every grower sees every greenhouse. The admin is left out of that list; the admin sees every greenhouse, including his own. Each card is a small shelf of up to three plant photos, with an empty slot for each one missing. Admin → Server users verifies up to 3 greenhouses for everyone. Home shows those in `TopGreenhouses` (hidden on the phone). News marks updates from those owners with the blue stamp and the verified-greenhouse label. Until an admin verifies, Home keeps the ranked top greenhouses.
- Greenhouse `CollectionGrid` is 2 columns under 560px of `Page`, with `AddPlantCard` first and infinite scroll on the filtered list. Cards in a row stretch to one height. The add tile stays cream like a plant card, the grid keeps start padding so that tile is not clipped, and hover scales it slightly. On a phone that tile keeps the spark and the hover scale, and shows “Add another plant” without the hint. No standalone Add button. No “Growing now” title and no search. Filter chips use `FilterChips`. Under 560px of `Page` they match the Tasks phone pills (13px, neutral fill, soft shadow, forest when selected) and wrap so All, AI verified, Needs care, and Upcoming stay full size. Wider than that they stay one scrolling row and snap so a chip is never left cut in half. Filter order: All, AI verified, Needs care, Upcoming, then Listed/Sold when market is ready. Needs care and Upcoming split into Watering and Picture sections (small headers); each is a 2-column grid under 560px of `Page` and 4 columns when wider, showing at most 8 cards with its own infinite scroll. Care cards show the due date and open `TodoCareDialog` (today’s day + that action) on click — complete water/photo there, no navigate to `/tasks`. Right rail is Greenhouse activities only (no expand/collapse, no “Needs you today” / `TodoTable` on greenhouse). On the mobile shell the rail is hidden; a bell in the top bar, left of the account bubble, opens that owner's activity. Feed cards, that activity thread, and Admin → Server activity rows share `ActivityMoment`. Each `FeedUpdateKind` tints the row and plays its animation (water is blue with ripples, a new plant is green and sways, an AI scan is lavender with no glyph). Under the kind, a feed row names the grower; demo persona labels show only in mock mode. Water, photo, propagate, listing, and scan open a tinted popup with the note and a preview `GreenhousePlantCard` (a scan also shows `PhotoChecks`). On a phone that popup is the same 75svh sheet with `SheetGrip`. Added, grade, and passport open `PassportDialog` — a new plant and a grade on grading, a passport update on the activity tab. Activity height locks to the bottom of the first plant-card row. Each activity row keeps its own height (`flex: none`) and the thread scrolls. On the phone bell the scrollbar stays hidden; older rows load as the thread scrolls up, and a cream fade with a moss hairline sits on the top edge while more activity is above. The shelf is the infinite-scroll scroller so the page and rail stay put — only on the wide split; once stacked (≤960px of `Page`) the page flows, the shelf is not a scroller, and `InfiniteSentinel` falls back to the viewport. On that wide split the shelf scrollbar stays hidden. While more plants sit below, a cream fade and a moss hairline rest on the bottom edge of the shelf. Under 560px card labels stay one line (ellipsis), the care date sits bottom-start, and care names clamp to two lines. A Growing well card uses the add-photo dashed border and growth pulse; hover, focus, and press turn that border solid and light the badge. On a wide split, a moss hairline divides shelf and Activity; the shelf keeps end padding so cards do not sit on the rule. No pane backgrounds. Home desktop hosts `TodoTable` ("Needs you today") at the bottom of the right rail, under the catalog: one tappable row per task with the plant photo, a water / photo badge, how late it is, and the action, tinted blue or peach; the phone sheet shows the same list. home mobile uses a floating Needs-today chip that opens the same table in a sheet, then `TodoCareDialog`.
- Tasks calendar (`TodoCalendar`): the page has no visible heading. On a phone (under 900px) the calendar is a Days chip (`TodoDayPicker`) in one row with All / Water / Photo, using that same pill. The plant picker is the width of the task list under them. The chip opens a short bottom sheet to pick days; OK fills those days and the list, titled Tasks, shows their tasks. With no days picked the list follows kind and plant. On a wider screen, All / Water / Photo and the plant select sit above the calendar card. There is no Today / Planned filter. The Tasks tab is a link, `Tasks (n)` with today's count. An empty greenhouse replaces those filters with the greenhouse `AddPlantCard` hero (add your first plant) — an undated mandatory task, not a calendar day. The Tasks tab label is `Tasks (n)` with today's count only (due today or overdue, first waterings, 1 for the first-plant task), and plain `Tasks` at 0; on the top bar and the bottom nav. Days multi-select only (no care popup on the grid — actions live in the panel under the calendar). Every day cell is one fixed row; a day with more than one task shows a count. A short hold opens the full set over that same cell and does not change the selection; a click selects the day, and a click while that set is open only closes it. The grid never grows. Deep link `/tasks/:id` still filters to that plant and selects its due day. On a phone, tapping a task opens the Days sheet for one day. Only today and the days back through that category’s gap can be chosen (water: the past week, photo: the past month), with a short line that the next task is scheduled from the day you pick. A task that is not due yet opens the same sheet with every day closed. Watering does not open the passport. On a wider screen, completing a task still drops the kind icon onto that day; a photo then opens `PassportDialog` on the Tasks tab, and watering does not. Passport Tasks (`PassportTodo` / `passport.todo`) lists planned and care history for that plant.

## Check

After a layout change, open the landing and the real route at a desktop width and a phone width. Confirm type is fully readable, Hebrew RTL still lines up, and landing frames stay inert.
