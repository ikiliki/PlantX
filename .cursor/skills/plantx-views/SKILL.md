# PlantX views

## Reuse

- Put UI in `src/components`, `src/features/<feature>/components`, or `src/pages`.
- Each component has a sibling `*.styles.ts` (styled-components) and a story.
- A landing mock, rail, or card uses the same component as the route. Do not draw a second fake version of a table, deck, feed, or catalog.
- The catalog is the wiki route (`/wiki`, feature id `wiki`). The tab label is Catalog. Its menu lists All, then rarity headings collapsed until clicked. The catalog page has no title block. The contents table starts collapsed. Under it, Common starts open and the other rarities stay closed until clicked. Home `WikiRail` stays off the phone (hidden under 900px of the home shell) and on the wider rails. Its rows open `CatalogPreview` (photos, this season’s light and water); the full seasons stay on the article. On a phone that preview is a 75svh sheet with `SheetGrip`.

## View

Shared type: `ComponentView` in `src/theme/view.ts` — `'page' | 'widget'`.

- `page` is the route. It can show the header, map, table of contents, and rails.
- `widget` is the same component in a smaller frame. Pass `view="widget"`. Show the same children, fewer rows, and drop chrome that only belongs on the route (page title block, map, toc).
- Default the prop to `'page'` so existing call sites stay full pages.
- The landing product section is feature copy plus stills from `public/landing/` (desk greenhouse, phone greenhouse, phone home). How steps crop those same stills. Do not mount a live page there.

`GradeStack` uses `tab | page | widget`. The widget deck is `min(320px, 100%)` wide with a 3:4 ratio. Do not size a deck with `100svh` inside a card.

## Size

- Layout that can sit in a card follows the container. Put `container-type: inline-size` on the parent and use `@container` on the children. A container does not query itself.
- Widths use `min(…px, 100%)`, `minmax(0, 1fr)`, and `min-width: 0`. Text wraps. Do not clip a title with `overflow: hidden` on a scaled page.
- A grid card that holds tables, chip rows, or previews sets `grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr)`; an implicit `auto` column lets one wide child push the card past the screen. Chip rows (`Segmented`, filters) stay one row and scroll sideways. Admin table expanded rows pin to the visible scroller (`ExpandBody`). A name in Server that points at another table opens a popup in place: a plant opens `PassportDialog`, a user opens the user preview. It does not scroll that other section open.
- Check phones with `scripts/mobile-audit.mjs` (mock dev server on 5174; 280–1000px, routes and dialogs); it lists anything poking past the viewport or its own card.
- Do not `transform: scale()` a full page into a frame. The landing used to render a viewport-wide tree and scale it; that clipped type and made container queries read the wrong ancestor.
- Viewport `@media` is only for the app shell (top bar, bottom nav). Embedded components use `@container`.
- Listing rows: the table scroller is the container. At `max-width: 720px` the row keeps photo, name, health, price, and change.
- Home columns follow `DiscoverPage` `Shell`. Below 900px the left rail hides; phones get a fixed, draggable `HomeMobileFloats` (`FloatChip`) for Needs you today — drag to move, tap opens a sheet popup (no dismiss). The greenhouse lure float is off the phone for now. Pinned greenhouses stay on the desktop home rail only. No inline MobileLure / MobileTodo / friends-world filter on the feed. `GreenhouseLure` always shows 3 shelf slots (plants, then empty + cards: 0→3, 1→2, 2→1, 3→0) and a compact `LoaderShell` while the plants slice is in flight.
- A grower popup (`GreenhousePublic` compact, from the news avatar or `/sellers/:id`) keeps the name header and lists that greenhouse in a vertical scroller. The window shows one fewer plant than they have, unless there are 0 or 1, and never more than 3. Go to greenhouse stays under the list on every width and opens `/greenhouse/:ownerId` — the public greenhouse page, not a profile. The name in the popup does not navigate. A plant in that list or on the public greenhouse opens `PassportDialog`.
- Market list and map follow `MarketPage` `Board` at 960px. Greenhouse and Tasks page headings are the name only; each hides under 720px of `Page` (the greenhouse wallet card stays when that placement is on).
- `GreenhouseWallet` is a cream card with market value and plant count. It mounts only while the market wallet placement is on. On a wide greenhouse `Page` the card is a header companion with a soft growth wash and a split divider; under 720px it stays the compact full-width card. When that placement is held, blur only the money.
- Plant passport stacks with `@container` on `Frame` (not viewport media), so a narrow dialog stacks on a wide window. On a phone the sheet is 75svh, with a `SheetGrip` on top; drag the pill down to close. `html` keeps `overflow-y: scroll` so locking scroll for any popup leaves the scrollbar in place and the page behind it does not widen. The photo is a sibling of the details and the activity. Under 760px those three stack in that order; the aside thumb hides; the AI badge is compact; the green wash runs top to bottom.
- Greenhouse scope is Mine or Global (`scope` query). On a phone the page shows a full-width leaf / globe toggle, split in half. From the desktop header, the Greenhouse tab dropdown carries Mine and Global. Global is search, then the verified greenhouses (always on top, even when the query misses them, with a blue `VerifiedStamp`), then the other grower cards loaded with `InfiniteSentinel`. Each card is a small shelf of up to three plant photos, with an empty slot for each one missing. Admin → Server users verifies up to 3 greenhouses for everyone. Home shows those in `TopGreenhouses` (hidden on the phone). News marks updates from those owners with the blue stamp and the verified-greenhouse label. Until an admin verifies, Home keeps the ranked top greenhouses.
- Greenhouse `CollectionGrid` is 2 columns under 560px of `Page`, with `AddPlantCard` first and infinite scroll on the filtered list. Cards in a row stretch to one height. The add tile stays cream like a plant card, the grid keeps start padding so that tile is not clipped, and hover scales it slightly. On a phone that tile keeps the spark and the hover scale, and shows “Add another plant” without the hint. No standalone Add button. No “Growing now” title and no search — filter chips stay on one scrolling row and snap so a chip is never left cut in half. Under 560px of `Page` that row runs edge to edge and All, AI verified, Needs care, and Upcoming sit fully on the one line. Filter order: All, AI verified, Needs care, Upcoming, then Listed/Sold when market is ready. Needs care and Upcoming split into Watering and Picture sections (small headers); each is a 2-column grid under 560px of `Page` and 4 columns when wider, showing at most 8 cards with its own infinite scroll. Care cards show the due date and open `TodoCareDialog` (today’s day + that action) on click — complete water/photo there, no navigate to `/tasks`. Right rail is Activity only (no expand/collapse, no “Needs you today” / `TodoTable` on greenhouse). On the mobile shell the rail is hidden; a bell in the top bar, left of the account bubble, opens that owner's activity. Feed cards, that activity thread, and Admin → Server activity rows share `ActivityMoment`. Each `FeedUpdateKind` tints the row and plays its animation (water is blue with ripples, a new plant is green and sways). Water, photo, propagate, listing, and scan open a tinted popup with the note and a preview `GreenhousePlantCard` (a scan also shows `PhotoChecks`). On a phone that popup is the same 75svh sheet with `SheetGrip`. Added, grade, and passport open `PassportDialog` — a new plant and a grade on grading, a passport update on the activity tab. Narrow filter chips scroll smoothly in one row. Activity height locks to the bottom of the first plant-card row. Each activity row keeps its own height (`flex: none`) and the thread scrolls. On the phone bell the scrollbar stays hidden; older rows load as the thread scrolls up, and a cream fade with a moss hairline sits on the top edge while more activity is above. The shelf is the infinite-scroll scroller so the page and rail stay put — only on the wide split; once stacked (≤960px of `Page`) the page flows, the shelf is not a scroller, and `InfiniteSentinel` falls back to the viewport. On that wide split the shelf scrollbar stays hidden. While more plants sit below, a cream fade and a moss hairline rest on the bottom edge of the shelf. Under 560px card labels stay one line (ellipsis), the care date sits bottom-start, and care names clamp to two lines. A Growing well card uses the add-photo dashed border and growth pulse; hover, focus, and press turn that border solid and light the badge. On a wide split, a moss hairline divides shelf and Activity; the shelf keeps end padding so cards do not sit on the rule. No pane backgrounds. Home desktop hosts `TodoTable` on the right rail; home mobile uses a floating Needs-today chip that opens the same table in a sheet, then `TodoCareDialog`.
- Tasks calendar (`TodoCalendar`): plant filter is a select; category chips (water / photo) stay on one row. An empty greenhouse replaces those filters with the greenhouse `AddPlantCard` hero (add your first plant) — an undated mandatory task, not a calendar day. The Tasks tab label is `Tasks (n)`: 1 for that first-plant task, otherwise the open-task count, on the top bar and the bottom nav. Days multi-select only (no care popup on the grid — actions live in the panel under the calendar). Every day cell is one fixed row; a day with more than one task shows a count. A short hold opens the full set over that same cell and does not change the selection; a click selects the day, and a click while that set is open only closes it. The grid never grows. Deep link `/tasks/:id` still filters to that plant and selects its due day. Completing a task drops the kind icon onto that day, then opens `PassportDialog` on the Tasks tab with `careMark` on the passport thumb. Passport Tasks (`PassportTodo` / `passport.todo`) lists planned and care history for that plant.

## Check

After a layout change, open the landing and the real route at a desktop width and a phone width. Confirm type is fully readable, Hebrew RTL still lines up, and landing frames stay inert.
